
**🍔 Food Delivery Analytics Dashboard**

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer behavior, satisfaction trends, demographic patterns, and business opportunities within a food delivery platform using Microsoft Power BI.
The project started with a raw food delivery dataset containing customer demographics, income categories, occupations, customer types, geographic locations, and customer feedback data.
Using Power BI, I performed data cleaning, data transformation, data modeling, KPI creation, and DAX calculations to transform raw data into actionable business insights.


🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to:
- Understand customer behavior patterns
- Measure customer satisfaction levels
- Identify key customer segments
- Analyze geographic distribution of customers
- Discover business growth opportunities
- Support data-driven decision making


🛠 Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence


📊 Dashboard Highlights
KPI Metrics

- Total Customers: 388
- Positive Feedback: 81.7%
- Negative Feedback: 18.3%
- Success Rate: 77.58%
Key Findings
✅ Regular Customers contribute the highest share of activity (56.19%)
✅ Customers aged 21–25 are the most active group
✅ Positive feedback dominates across most customer segments
✅ Students represent the largest customer category
✅ Customer activity is concentrated in key Bengaluru locations


💡 Business Recommendations

- Introduce loyalty programs for regular customers
- Launch targeted campaigns for the 21–25 age group
- Provide student-focused discounts and meal plans
- Improve service based on negative feedback trends
- Optimize delivery operations in high-demand locations
